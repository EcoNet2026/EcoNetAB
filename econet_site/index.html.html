🏢 About EcoNet AB

EcoNet AB is a Swedish private limited company that provides communication, dissemination, event, ecosystem development, and consulting services for projects and organizations operating within research, innovation, digitalization, information technology, and societal development in Sweden. The company focuses particularly on projects with external financing, such as national and EU-funded research and innovation initiatives. EcoNet AB is 100% owned and operated by one individual, who acts as Managing Director, sole board member, and authorized signatory.
